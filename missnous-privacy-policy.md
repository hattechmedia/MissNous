## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember your cart and preferences, understand how the Site is used, and, where permitted, support marketing.

- **Essential cookies** keep the Site working, such as holding items in your cart and enabling checkout.
- **Analytics cookies** help us understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it.
- **Marketing cookies**, where used, help us measure and tailor advertising.

Where required by law, we ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time through our cookie banner or your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies, though some features of the Site may not work properly if you do.
